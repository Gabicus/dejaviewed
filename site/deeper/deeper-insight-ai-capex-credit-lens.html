An ex-Morgan Stanley structurer explains that CoreWeave-style GPU loans have a thin tail: loan, contract and GPU useful life are nearly the same length, so the bull case rests on hourly rental prices after contracts end. Token deflation is a direct input to post-contract GPU rental rates, and nobody in the catalog connects them.
