Editor's take

In these posts TouchDesigner is the real-time engine for interactive art, and the input is usually a body. MediaPipe appears in 6 of these posts, almost always through @blankensmithing's plugin, which @neles0n tries out on camera (MediaPipe plugin). The clearest example is @pepepepebrick's piece where hand tracking steers two digital spiders over a generated 3D web, with leg count and spider distance mapped to pitch and waveform in VCV Rack (spider web synth). Other inputs appear too: @pala3d routes Kinect body data through TouchDesigner into Unreal Engine to drive particle views of NASA and CERN datasets (DATA CONTROL), and @the.poet.engineer mocks up a non-functional gesture interface where a pinch delegates work to an agent (gestural agent UI).

The technique posts go deeper than the demos. @as.ws__ explains storing parametric equations in image pixels, since color is a vector, so one texture can hold many equations that build forms like jellyfish (equations as images). At DATLAB, @blankensmithing and @dotsimulate present free components for training classifiers on any CHOP data, from gestures to EEG, and point out the classifier is a search algorithm rather than machine learning (Classifier TOX). 3D scanning with Polycam and Gaussian splats show up as companion techniques (creative coding guide).

The gaps are mostly in what the posts leave out: many are silent process clips with no patch shown, such as a circular light array (ARRAY). @ojrgb voices mixed feelings about "Claude can do this in 10 minutes" captions watering down a deep craft (mixed feelings). The Gemini Omni news tag is incidental. The full hand-tracking setup is collected in the MediaPipe to TouchDesigner pipeline. It fits installation artists and creative coders comfortable with node-based patching.
