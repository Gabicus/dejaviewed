Editor's take

This hub merges posts tagged ChatGPT, GPT-5, GPT-4 and GPT-4o, and many of the 47 mention ChatGPT only in passing. The posts that actually center on it are mostly prompt craft: a bank of five study prompts such as an 80/20 learning plan and a 30-day skill plan (learning prompts), a breakdown of OpenAI's GPT-5 structure of role, task, context, reasoning, output format and stop condition (GPT-5 prompt anatomy), and JSON profiles that make image prompts repeatable (JSON image profiles). Marketers upload a competitor ad to GPT-4o, ask for a component-by-component breakdown, then regenerate it with their own product photo (ad deconstruction). More of this lives in the prompt engineering guide.

A second cluster treats ChatGPT as a destination rather than a tool. One shop owner shipped an llms.txt file so ChatGPT would recommend his wrap business (llms.txt), while another post warns that quality publishers block GPTBot, so chatbot citations lean on AI-written pages and listicles, and advises clicking every citation (AI search citations). A red-team post reports a public jailbreak prompt library still landing on GPT-5 (jailbreak test).

On value, an opinionated tier list says that at $20 a month ChatGPT or Gemini give more breadth than the Claude chatbot (tier list). Claude Code shows up in 7 of these posts, Gemini and Codex in 4 each, DeepSeek in 3; one setup runs GPT-5.6 through the Codex plugin inside Claude Code as the executor while Claude plans and reviews (routing). Several recent GPT-6 Astra posts are sponsored or comment-gated with no usable content (Astra bait), so the useful material here is for people who write prompts daily and want structure, not news.
