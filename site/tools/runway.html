Editor's take

Runway's footprint here is small: 5 posts, once four that only used the word "runway" (a fashion runway, a song lyric) are left out. The hands-on posts date from 2024 and center on Gen-3 Alpha. @benkaluza paid $15 for the standard tier right after Gen-3 Alpha went public and found that a surreal macro prompt, horned puppies on a fingertip, took a few generations to land; his takeaway is to budget credits for retries (Gen-3 first test). Gen-3 Alpha Video to Video is presented as a control for movement and expression in generations (Video to Video). @realrileybrown describes a node canvas where AI fills the frames between keyframes and branches alternate versions instead of following a linear Premiere or CapCut timeline; at the time it was a demo (non-linear video).

Later posts treat Runway as one interchangeable video model. @nateherkai's synthetic-influencer breakdown says to animate "with an AI video model like Veo or Runway" (synthetic creator), and a Gemini Omni news piece groups it with Veo, Sora and Kling as diffusion-only tools (Gemini Omni). ChatGPT, Sora and Veo each appear in 2 of these posts, mostly as comparisons rather than pipeline partners. Broader context is in AI video production. Runway suits video makers who want image-to-video and keyframe control, with the caveat that this archive holds little recent hands-on testing of it.
