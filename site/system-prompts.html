How to adapt one. Every prompt here follows the same five-part shape, and you can rewrite each part for your own job. Start with the role and stance: name the expert the agent should act as and what it refuses to trust, such as blog posts over the installed version's own docs. Then set the constraints: versions to pin, budgets, files it must not touch, and what it may not claim without proof. Next comes the deliverable: the exact files, folders or reports to produce, so done is checkable. Add the audit: after each step, the agent re-reads its output against the step's goal and fixes gaps before moving on. Finish with stop conditions: the tests or outside checks that must pass, and when to stop and ask instead of guessing. Keep your edits concrete. Name real paths, versions and numbers in place of adjectives.
