AI video generators: what creators actually use

As of 22 October 2026, Higgsfield is the one creators in our corpus use most, because it bundles Kling, Seedance, Veo and others behind one plan and plugs into Claude through MCP. If you want one model directly, Kling 4.0 and Seedance 2.5 make the longest single clips at 30 seconds, Runway Gen-4.5 has the simplest API price at $0.12 a second, and Google's Gemini Omni is the cheapest way in at $4.99 a month [17].

What changed recently

  • Sora is gone. OpenAI closed the Sora app on 26 April 2026 and shut the Sora 2 API on 24 September 2026, with no replacement [21] [22]. Some platforms still show old Sora pages [1].
  • Gemini Omni replaced Veo in Google's consumer apps. Omni 1.1 Flash shipped on 27 August 2026 [11]. The Veo 3.1 previews on the Gemini API shut down on 22 October 2026, and Veo 3.1 on Google Cloud retires on or after 17 November 2026 [16].
  • Kling 4.0 launched on 15 October 2026 with 30-second single clips. Its API is not live yet [8] [10].

What creators used each one for

We read every saved post in the DejaViewed corpus that shows one of these tools in use and grouped them by tool. Posts that only mention a tool in passing are left out. One general tip holds across all of them: seed text-to-video with a starting image for more reliable results (Text-to-video tip: start from an image — @setupspawn).

Which one to pick

Start with Higgsfield if you want to try several models without several subscriptions, or if you drive production from Claude. Pick Kling or Seedance directly when you need long single shots and will generate enough to justify their own plans. Pick Runway if you edit and iterate more than you generate, or want a plain per-second API. Pick Google Veo / Gemini Omni if you already pay for Google AI or want the cheapest start.

Treat this as creator evidence, not a benchmark. We have not yet run the same prompt through every tool, and most of the posts behind it predate Kling 4.0, Seedance 2.5 and Gemini Omni.

What we could not verify

  • Whether Runway Gen-4.5 generates audio natively: one Runway page says yes, and the Gen-4.5 help spec does not list it.
  • Per-second API prices for Gemini Omni and Seedance are derived from published token prices, not quoted per second by the vendor.
  • Google Cloud lists Veo prices per "count"; we read that as per second.
  • Seedance 2.5's release date (reported as 31 July 2026 by third parties) and its limit of 50 reference assets per request.
  • The daily free-credit amount on Kling's Basic plan, which the plan page does not display.
  • Whether Higgsfield's older DoP model still exists; it no longer appears on the site.
