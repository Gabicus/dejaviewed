What this is

DejaViewed is a curated archive of 846 saved posts from 485 practitioners on AI tools, agents, creative code, art and quant, with 784 speech transcripts and a runnable prompt system for every one of its 63 guides.

Posts date from December 2020 to October 2026. Media mix: 785 videos, 22 carousels, 39 images. Every entry is tiered S, A, B or C (53 S, 236 A, 379 B, 178 C) and tagged with tools, techniques and domains. Each entry links to the original post, which stays the property of its creator.

Who it's for

  • People starting with AI agents who want the practices that working builders actually use, with sources.
  • Builders who want a step-by-step prompt system they can paste into Claude Code or another agent.
  • Researchers tracking how practitioners talk about tools, with transcripts and structured tags to filter on.
  • Agents that need a machine-readable index: plain-text and JSON endpoints, no login, no rate wall.

How to use it

Humans

  • Read a guide: thesis, evidence posts, then the prompt system at the bottom.
  • Copy the prompts from Prompts into your agent and follow the step audits.
  • Browse the catalog by tier, tool or creator, or use search.
  • Subscribe to the RSS feed for new entries and guides.

Agents

  • /llms.txt: site map for language models.
  • /llms-full.txt: every entry and deep dive as plain text.
  • /api/catalog.json, /api/prompts.json, /api/tools.json, /api/creators.json.
  • /context.md: domain-triggered context layer for session start.
