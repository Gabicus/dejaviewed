50k ft — Why care?

Pull the top 10 results for 72 keywords and have TypeSafe's Jev (a classifier model that returns probabilities) answer literal questions per result: intent match, depth, freshness, site strength, beatable or not. Three or more weak results means target the keyword, then Claude writes. 720 results cost about 4 cents, search volume still needs a separate tool.

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Ask literal yes/no questions for clean classifier answers • Manually review results the model was unsure about • Pick keywords by how beatable page one is, not by volume • Three or more weak top-10 results marks a winnable keyword

5k ft — Tools & techniques

Claude, Jev LLM-as-classifier, SERP scoring

1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

Comment “JEV” for the full guide. Everyone’s writing blog posts. Almost nobody checks who they’re up against before they write. So I pulled the top 10 search results for 72 keywords in my niche. 720 real results. Then set Jev up to check which ones are actually beatable. Here’s the setup. Pick your keywords. Things your buyers actually search. Pull the top 10 results for each one. Ask Jev the same questions about every result. Does it match what the searcher wants. How deep is the content. How fresh is it. How strong is the site. Could you beat it. Thin pages, old posts, forum threads and off-topic results light up orange.
