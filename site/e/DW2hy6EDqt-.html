50k ft — Why care?

Walkthrough of Kronos, a free time-series foundation model from a Tsinghua PhD student that forecasts stocks and crypto. It tokenizes K-lines (OHLC plus volume) into discrete tokens and trains an autoregressive model on them, so it generalizes like an LLM over market data.
LLMcryptofinance/tradingquantvideo production

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Candles need a numeric-aware tokenizer, not word tokenization. • Kronos is open and pretrained, so you can fine-tune or backtest it directly. • Treat forecasts as a feature to validate, not a signal to trust.

5k ft — Tools & techniques

Kronos K-line tokenization, algorithmic trading, autoregressive forecasting, time series foundation model
