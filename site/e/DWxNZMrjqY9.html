50k ft — Why care?

USTC researchers in Hefei built a quantum reservoir computer from nine interacting atoms that beat classical baselines on weather prediction. The reservoir needs no training, only the readout is trained, and quantum noise is used as the fading memory that time-series forecasting needs.
