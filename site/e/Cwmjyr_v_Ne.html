techniqueS-tier

50k ft — Why care?

A traditional hon-kintsugi repair of a broken tokkuri (sake flask): crack-stopping and interior repairs are finished first because the narrow neck blocks later access, then shards are glued with mugi-urushi, squeeze-out is trimmed with a design knife, and seams are finished in keshifun gold powder.
artdesign

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Keshifun gold powder gives a soft matte finish on the seams • Mugi-urushi (wheat-flour lacquer) is the adhesive; trim squeeze-out by hand • On narrow-necked vessels, repair the interior before bonding the shards

5k ft — Tools & techniques

keshifun gold powder, urushi lacquer hon-kintsugi, mugi-urushi bonding
