1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

You can start selling prints of a painting you already own tonight. No printer, no stock, no money up front. Here’s the bit nobody explains. There are companies that print and post the thing for you. You upload a photo, you set your price, and when someone orders, they print it, pack it, ship it and send you the difference. You never touch a box. Printful, Gelato and Fine Art America all work roughly like this and none charge you to open an account. So the only real job is the photograph: Flat, in daylight, near a window. Overcast is better than sun. No flash. Phone straight on, propped on books if you’ve no tripod. Not at an angle. Crop to the edge of the canvas.
