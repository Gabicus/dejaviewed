50k ft — Why care?

Cites research showing that across global markets most equity returns come overnight (close to next open) while intraday holding loses; the Micron example claims +138M% overnight vs −99% intraday on paper. Proposed mechanism: retail trades the open, institutions trade into the close. Paper not named; frictions and costs ignored.

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Close-to-open holding is a testable edge; costs and gap risk decide if it survives. • Decompose any strategy's returns into overnight and intraday legs. • Look up the 'tug of war' overnight-returns literature before trusting the headline numbers.
