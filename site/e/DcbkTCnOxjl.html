Transcript excerpt
If you bought Micron every day when the stock market closed and sold it the next morning when it opened, you'd be up over 138 million percent on paper. Now imagine you do the opposite. You bought every day when the market opens and you sold when it closed. That same Micron trade would be down over 99%. And apparently this isn't the only stock that does this. A research paper found a weird phenomenon across multiple markets across the world. It basically shows that if you buy stocks when the market closes and sell it the next morning when it opens, you're statistically most likely to gain profit. So what's going on? Two different crowds are trading at two different times. Individuals tend to trade smaller positions at the open while big institutions trade during the day into the close because that's where the most liquidity is.
