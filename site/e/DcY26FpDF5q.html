1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

Grace Hopper's predictions for computing, made back in 1982. Full talk linked in bio.
Transcript excerpt
No work, no research has been done on the value of information. We've completely failed to look at it, and yet it's going to make a tremendous difference in how we run our computer systems of the future. Because if there are two things that are dead sure, I don't even have to call them predictions, one is that the amount of data and the amount of information will continue to increase, and it's more than linear, and the other is the demand for instant access to that information will increase, and those two are in conflict. We've got to know something about the value of the information being processed.
