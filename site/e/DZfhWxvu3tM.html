50k ft — Why care?

ASCILINE converts real video server-side into binary-packed text sent over WebSockets, and the browser repaints colored block characters on a canvas at 360p/30fps. No video element, so it slips past video blockers and works on weak connections; frames can also be piped to local LLMs.
creative codingopen sourcevideo production

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Useful pattern for low-bandwidth or terminal-style visuals • Video can be delivered as text frames with no media element • link: https://github.com/YusufB5/ASCILINE

1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

ASCILINE plays real 360p video at 30fps… but there’s no video element on the page Ships with: • Server converts real video → binary packed text over WebSockets • Browser paints it as colored block characters fast enough to look like video • Completely bypasses ad blockers and video blockers (no `` tag) • Extremely lightweight — works on terrible internet • Bonus: CSS effects, copy-paste frames, or feed directly to local LLMs 👉 https://github.com/YusufB5/ASCILINE
