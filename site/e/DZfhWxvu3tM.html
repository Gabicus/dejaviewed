50k ft — Why care?

ASCILINE converts real video server-side into binary-packed text sent over WebSockets, and the browser repaints colored block characters on a canvas at 360p/30fps. No video element, so it slips past video blockers and works on weak connections; frames can also be piped to local LLMs.
creative codingopen sourcevideo production
