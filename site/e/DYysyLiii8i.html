50k ft — Why care?

Built entirely with Claude Code: a probe sweeps iPhone clips of a room, locks onto objects and samples dominant color, luminance and position for 42 captures, then redraws them as a living color map and 3D data. Part of a workflow for turning taste and mood boards into reusable design-system data.
