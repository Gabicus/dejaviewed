1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

AI-designed viruses sound terrifying, but these were bacteriophages, meaning viruses that infect bacteria. A phage is just short for bacteriophage. A genome is the complete set of genetic instructions inside an organism or virus. A mosaic descendant is a later virus whose genome contains pieces inherited from multiple parent phages after recombination. Researchers used AI to design new phage genomes, tested them in the lab, and found viable designs. Some later recombined into mosaic descendants during experiments. We are now seeing specialized AI models being used to build complex, interrelated systems!
