You can paste a whole product requirement stock and get a full clickable prototype in minutes. This one's my favorite, you can generate multiple versions of the same screen at the same time. Instead of designing one direction and hoping that it's right, Figma Mate gives you four different design options that you can compare side by side. I use this constantly because it allows me to pick one that I like, iterate on that, and move on. What you should take me a full day of exploration takes me about 10 minutes now. Figma Mate pulls from your actual components, your brand colors, and your typography, which means that every single output that you make flows with how your product looks, instead of looking like generic AI at output.
