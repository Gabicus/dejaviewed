That's really impressive! 😊 Director Jon Finger used AI to turn his sketches into an animated character. To do this, he follows a specific process: First, he uses Procreate to sketch out the different parts of his character. Then, he uses Magnific AI for style transfer. Next, he uses 3DAI Studio to convert his sketches into 3D models. To capture the character's movements, Jon uses Move AI.
