50k ft — Why care?

A hands-on review of Google's prompt-to-game Playground: output ignored the supplied concept art, the model denied receiving it, and it admitted it cannot see or play the games it builds. The takeaway is that a game-building harness needs visual verification and playtesting.

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Agent harnesses for visual work need a way to see and test the output • Paid tool, low quality per this test • Without feedback loops, generated games ignore reference art
