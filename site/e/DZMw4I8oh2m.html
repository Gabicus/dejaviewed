50k ft — Why care?

A tight template: sentence one says what you make in plain English ("I make large oil paintings of empty rooms"), two says why this subject and nothing else, three names only the process that changes the meaning, and four states what you want viewers to take away. Cut the copied art-speak. The creator also sells a book with templates.

10k ft — Key takeaways

• If a stranger cannot picture the work from sentence one, rewrite it • Mention process only when it changes what the work says • The why sentence is the one collectors remember

Your artist statement only needs four sentences. One. What you make, in plain English. Two. Why this and nothing else, the question you keep circling. Three. Most statements are a paragraph of art speak copied from someone who copied it from someone else, and they say nothing. Yours is not weak because you are not clever enough.
