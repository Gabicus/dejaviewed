So this video just went viral, yay! And I want to show you guys the exact workflow I used to make this project. The first thing I did is jump into Claude and using their chat feature, I started brainstorming and discussing my idea. I wanted it to ask me questions and I didn't want it to start building anything yet. The second thing I do, which I think is very important, is understanding which tools Claude will be using. This helps you later on explain to Claude any areas where it might be not understanding what you're trying to build and problem solve. The third step is always asking for an interactive prototype within the chat before you start building anything. After I'm really happy with the interactive prototype, I ask Claude to give me a detailed prompt about what it's trying to build. Once I have all of that, I take the prompt and move it over to co-work. The reason I move it over to co-work is because co-work has access to my terminal, has access to my computer, and it can execute everything without me touching a finger. Once Claude got everything up and running, it gives you a HTML file, and then from there you'll be able to access your camera and see how the full project is working. I hope this work will help. Let me know if you have any other questions. Bye.
