1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

💻🧠 The team behind Claude Code just shared they actually uses it internally and why many new users struggle at the start. In a recent thread on X, Boris Cherny explained that the team’s workflow looks very different from how most people approach the tool. Instead of running one session, they often spin up 3 to 5 git worktrees in parallel, each with its own Claude session, which they describe as a major productivity unlock. They also begin every complex task in plan mode and focus heavily on writing a strong plan before implementation. If something breaks or drifts off course, they go back to planning rather than forcing a fix. Another key habit is updating Claude’s own rules after mistakes so the same errors do not repeat.
