50k ft — Why care?

At GTC Taipei, NVIDIA unveiled RTX Spark, an ARM-based system-on-chip for Windows. It combines a 20-core CPU, a Blackwell GPU and up to 128 GB of unified memory on one chip, so large models can run locally, and more than 30 laptops and 10 desktops have been announced. Trade-offs include slower memory than discrete setups and Prism emulation for x86 apps.
