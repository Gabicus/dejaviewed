So we got your test results back. I'm afraid you're artistic. I'm autistic? No, no, no, no. You're not artistic. There's nothing wrong with that. You're artistic. There is something wrong with that. Oh. What's the prognosis? Well, that's where things get somewhat complicated. You see, you live roughly the same length as the average person, but it's your quality of life. That'll be a concern. How's my quality of life going to change? Well, where do I start? You'll have a lot of hobbies that you'll fail to monetize. You'll dabble in a thousand things, but never fully commit to one. You'll always be somewhat distracted. Do you hear that noise? Is that your air conditioner really the noise? It'll be difficult for you to finish anything. People will try to take advantage of you and pay you an exposure. Yeah, I think your air conditioner is humming like a C sharp or something. Daniel, focus. Being artistic is hard. In success, you'll be suspicious of your friends. In failure. You'll be suspicious of your dreams.
