1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

For those of you who’ve been following awhile you know I’ve been working hard on creating a book. I counted how many dives I’ve done for this project, it’s 83. In these images I wanted show alittle bit of my process creating these images. 2 years ago @alivelikethewind and I road-tripped through the Yucatán peninsula searching for cenotes to shoot in. During that time we took this photo (image 2). When researching for my book I used alot of my photos I had already taken in the cenotes as reference. Reshooting them with better knowledge of the area as well as better fitting the overarching theme I have been working towards.
