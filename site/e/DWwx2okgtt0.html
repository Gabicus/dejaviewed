1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

A simple tweak in how you talk to AI can quietly change how much you pay for it. One user tested this by forcing Claude to respond in extremely short, stripped-down sentences, almost like caveman-style language, removing filler, structure, and anything unnecessary. Because AI pricing is based on tokens, fewer words directly mean lower cost, and in this case, it cut usage by up to 75 percent without breaking the core output. The model still completes the task, but it does it with less explanation, less polish, and almost no extra wording, just the result.
