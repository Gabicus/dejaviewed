1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

There’s a twelve hundred dollar grant with no proposal, no plan, and no artist statement. You email them pictures. That’s the whole application. It’s called Artist Grant. Running since 2017, they give $1,200 unrestricted to one visual artist three times a year. Unrestricted meaning nobody tells you what to spend it on. The actual application: you send an email. In it, either a link to your website, or 3 to 5 images of your work plus a short bio. You tell them whether they can share your work on their social media. That’s it. Their own site says, and I’m quoting, no grant proposal required. Open to visual artists anywhere in the world, 18 and over.
