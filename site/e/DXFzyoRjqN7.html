50k ft — Why care?

ASI-Evolve, from Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Shanghai Innovation Institute, is an open-source agentic framework that automates AI research in a learn-design-experiment-analyze loop across data, architecture and learning algorithms. The paper reports 105 autonomously discovered architectures, with the best beating DeltaNet. The video also touches on China's AGI framing in its five-year plan.
LLMagentsnews

10k ft — Key takeaways

• A closed learn-design-experiment-analyze loop can search data, architecture and optimizer choices together. • Open code means you can try the loop on your own small-model experiments. • Read the paper's DeltaNet comparison before believing the 'tripled human gains' claim.
