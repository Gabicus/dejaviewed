Stop treating every market environment exactly the same. 🛑 ​Most traders lose money because they apply a trending strategy in a ranging market, or a mean-reversion strategy during a massive breakout. ​Enter: Hidden Markov Models (HMMs). 🧠 ​Instead of relying on lagging indicators, this machine learning model analyzes observable data (like returns and volatility) to mathematically deduce the "hidden" state of the market: 🟢 State 1: Low Volatility Bull Market 🔴 State 2: High Volatility Bear Market 🟡 State 3: Sideways / Ranging ​When you know the regime, you know exactly which strategy to deploy.
