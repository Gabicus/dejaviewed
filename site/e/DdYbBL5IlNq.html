1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

Glenn Gould was one of the great pianists of the twentieth century, and he sang while he played. Not quietly. A wordless humming that followed whatever line his hands were on, and it is there on almost every record he made. Columbia’s engineers spent years trying to get rid of it. They pulled the microphones further from his face, changed the angles, tried putting a screen between him and the keyboard. Nothing worked. There was nowhere to put a microphone that could hear the piano but not the man bent over it. Gould never treated it as a fault. He said he was not so much playing the music as thinking it, and the voice was part of the thinking.
