1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

A simple reversal strategy on crypto: If the price goes up, go short and If the price goes down, go long. Dollar-neutral means total longs equals total shorts. Positions are scaled so total absolute exposure stays equals 1, keeping risk consistent. Without considering any transaction costs, the results seem good. With transaction costs, however, the returns vanish quickly. But I found this is still a good start when learning before starting to explore other signals and strategies.
