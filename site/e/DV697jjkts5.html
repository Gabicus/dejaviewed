50k ft — Why care?

tiat, short for 'the intersection of art and technology', is a San Francisco 501(c)(3) nonprofit. It offers co-working for creative technologists from noon to 6pm on weekdays, free for members or a $10 day pass, plus exhibitions, workshops and salons.
