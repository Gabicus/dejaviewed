50k ft — Why care?

Xiaomi showed a prototype AI Cube with 80GB unified memory running 3B and 120B models on-device, built on three in-house chips: XRING O3 (mobile), O100 AI accelerator (claimed 1.22 TB/s near-memory bandwidth) and D100 3nm driving chip. No price, release date or independent benchmarks yet.

1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

Xiaomi just showed an Al Cube prototype with 80GB of unified memory running a 120-billion-parameter model locally. The wild part: Xiaomi designed the chips inside it too. The ring 03 comes from its mobile devices. The 0100 is built for Al and Xiaomi says it reaches 1.22 TB/s of near-memory bandwidth. The D100 was designed for intelligent driving. So Xiaomi is now designing chips for phones, cars, and Al, then combining them inside entirely new kinds of computers.
