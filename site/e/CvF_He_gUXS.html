50k ft — Why care?

An artist stains raw wood picture frames with India ink (Speedball) to get the thin black-metal-frame look. It dries almost instantly and, unlike stain, doesn't smell.
artdesign

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Cheap way to get a gallery-style thin black frame for your own work. • India ink works as a quick, odorless black stain on wood frames.
