50k ft — Why care?

Have an agent scrape journalist request sites into a database, pull each journalist's last ten articles, draft a similar story angle featuring you, and send short emails. Lessons: short non-AI-sounding pitches win, offer visibility, do ideation yourself, claim your Google profile, and it's a volume game.

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Give the model example emails so it doesn't sound like AI • Pitch angles close to what the journalist already writes • Short emails get more replies than long pitches

1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

After testing this here are my main findings: 1. Really short emails get far more replies than long pitches. Try really hard to make it not read like AI. The best way to do this is to provide examples of exactly what the email should look like. 2. The fact that I have some followers on ig and TikTok definitely helped get replies lol. These journalists also want to be famous so framing it in a “I will get you more visibility” way certainly worked. 3. AI is going to be bad at writing stories so actually try to put some work into ideation yourself. 4. You apparently have to actually claim the Google profile thing.
