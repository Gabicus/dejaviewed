50k ft — Why care?

Graffiti artist Victoriano spray-painted 29 sequential frames of a Michael Jordan dunk over a few days in the studio and shot them as a stop-motion loop. A clean example of 'motion graffiti': each frame is a full mural, the animation is the edit.

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Frame-by-frame painting on one wall can be shot as stop-motion. • ~29 frames is enough for a readable short loop of a single action.
