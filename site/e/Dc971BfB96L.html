In 1977, Dolly Parton invited her parents, Robert Lee and Avie Lee, and seven of her siblings onto her variety show to perform “In the Pines,” an old Appalachian folk song the family had sung together growing up in East Tennessee. They performed several songs together that day, offering a rare glimpse behind Dolly’s enormous career. Before the records, awards and worldwide fame, music was something the Parton family shared at home — and watching them sing together shows exactly where so much of Dolly’s musical foundation began.
