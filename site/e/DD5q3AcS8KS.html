Alright, we have a new AI model that's absolutely insane. It turns any image into a fully-explorable 3D world. Explorer by Odyssey just dropped and even Pixar's co-founder, Ed Capmull, joined the board. This completely changes everything for film and gaming. The way it works is genius. Feed it any image and Explorer transforms it into a complete, detailed 3D environment using Gaussian Splats. The quality is unreal. What makes it different, the worlds are fully editable. You can load them straight into Unreal, Blender, Houdini, and customize everything. They've already tested it at Garden Studios for actual film production. The worlds look so realistic on their virtual production stage that they're using it for real shoots. Right now generations take about 10 minutes, but they're working on real-time generation and even video-to-world capabilities. They're slowly rolling it out to artists who will push the technology.
