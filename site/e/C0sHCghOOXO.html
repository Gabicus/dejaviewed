John Prine & Iris DeMent - “In Spite of Ourselves” Today we’re looking back on a duet between two legendary figures of folk music. John Prine wrote this humorous track while recovering from radiation therapy and surgery on his throat. In a 2016 interview, he recounted requesting Iris Dement to join him on the track, “I kinda had her voice in mind when I was doing it because I know Iris can be a little sassy and she could tear this guy down… I told Iris, ‘I want you to listen to this and just see. It’s got a few questionable things in it that you’re gonna have to say. So you just tell me whether you would want to do it or not. We’ll leave it at that... So I didn’t hear from her for three weeks. Nothing. Finally, I called her and I said, “Iris?” And she said, “Yes?” I said, “Well?” And she…
