1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

GeoSpy can figure out where a photo was taken from just a few pixels. It scans the image for clues like buildings, plants, street layouts and shadows. Then it compares those clues to a massive database of geotagged images. The AI narrows it down using patterns in climate, architecture and even the angle of sunlight until it can pinpoint a location with surprising accuracy.
