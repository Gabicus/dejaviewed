Their debut single, “Highwayman,” became an instant No. 1 hit and launched a hugely successful tour that introduced fans to one of the greatest supergroups in country music history. Each member had already built a legendary solo career, but together they represented the rebellious spirit of outlaw country, earning the respect of both fans and fellow musicians while leaving behind one of the most iconic legacies in country music. #outlawcountry
