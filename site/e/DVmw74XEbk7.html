I just made my Cloud Code, my social media manager. Here's how I did it and the two most important things that it's doing to help me grow my platforms. I first installed this appify MCP, which allows you to collect information about any video or channel on almost any social media platform. I then use my Cloud Code to point it at all of my social media's to download and transcribe all of my post and videos to collect information about myself. I'm then using Cloud Code to identify trends, phrases, and scripts that people connect with and those that they do not. Now, what this is allowing me to do is simulate what will happen if I upload certain types or pieces of content and helps me weed out the ones that won't get attention or that are straight up bad at a much higher accuracy than if I was just doing the guesswork myself.
