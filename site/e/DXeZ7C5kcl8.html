The first one is one that everyone's asking about, what I call my good morning text. This is my to-do agent that runs every morning at 8am before I wake up. It's connected to my Google Calendar, Gmail, and Notion. Scans my calendar, and Gmail tells me what I have to do for the day, what I have to respond to, and organizes it in my Notion databases. Then it just sends me a text on my message, so I wake up with exactly what I need. Second one is my leads agent. This one runs every morning at 9am just to start the week. It finds three new potential clients tell me what the company does, audits their entire product, drafts cold emails, saves it to my Notion database, as well as my Gmail draft. So then when I wake up, I can take a look, make sure everything looks good, and set down the email. The third one is my communications agent. This one is on demand, so it's not scheduled. It has all of my information, the way I talk, and address proposals, SOWs, emails, whatever written text thing that I need from call notes. Fourth is a filing agent. This one is also on demand, because I just do a cleanup once in a while. It scans the folders that I gave access to, it source files into folders, supply naming conventions, and flags duplicates.
