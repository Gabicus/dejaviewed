50k ft — Why care?

Meituan, a food-delivery company, released LongCat 2.0. It's a 1.6-trillion-parameter model with a 1M-token context window, first released anonymously on OpenRouter as 'Owl Alpha'. Training ran entirely on domestic chips, and analysts trace the collective-communications layer to Huawei's HCCL, a replacement for NVIDIA's NCCL. It reportedly beats GPT-5.5 on some coding benchmarks.
