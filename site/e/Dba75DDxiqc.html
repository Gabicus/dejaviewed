50k ft — Why care?

Argues against fully AI faceless-channel courses: have an agent scrape a horror subreddit (with credit), generate clips with a video model, then hire a human narrator and editor (or use Descript). AI handles admin and sourcing, humans keep the creative part.

• AI voiceover hurts retention, real narrators win • Use agents for sourcing and hiring, not the creative voice
