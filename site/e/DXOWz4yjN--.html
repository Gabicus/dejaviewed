“Claude can do this in 10 minutes!” Is a caption I see a LOT rn and I definitely have mixed feelings about it. On one hand it’s awesome seeing so many people experience the magic of software engineering. On the other hand I am (a little?) concerned about the watering down of what is essentially a very complicated magic trick to a “caricature” of what it really is. I get my kicks out of feeling like I pushed the envelope and (maybe?) that gives me the appreciation for the folks who laid the foundation and did the research that enabled me to do what I do.
