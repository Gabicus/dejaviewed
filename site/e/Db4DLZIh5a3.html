50k ft — Why care?

High-credibility sites (Consumer Reports, ~60% of top news) block GPTBot, Google's AI crawler and Common Crawl, so AI search reads only what's left: AI-written pages and self-ranking listicles. One in six cited sources is AI-written and models often do post-hoc citation, writing the answer first and finding sources after.
LLMbrandingnews

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Click every AI citation and check the answer is actually on that page • Quality publishers disallow AI crawlers in robots.txt, so AI search skews to low-quality sources • Treat listicles cited by AI as possibly brand self-ranking

5k ft — Tools & techniques

ChatGPT, Claude post-hoc citation
