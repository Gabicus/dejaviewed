resourceA-tier

50k ft — Why care?

Walkthrough of Booking.com's GenAI telemetry architecture: apps emit traces to a collector, which normalizes them onto a managed Kafka bus (durable buffer decoupling producers and consumers), through a dedicated PII-redaction service that masks span attributes and text, then out to Arize for real-time monitoring and a warehouse for offline analytics and backfills. A second pipeline ships ML prediction logs to Arize for ranking and fraud models.
architecturedata sciencesecuritysoftware development

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Dual-export to a monitoring tool and a warehouse for long-term trends. • Make PII redaction a service in the pipeline, not a policy. • Put a durable message bus between AI apps and observability vendors.

5k ft — Tools & techniques

Arize, Kafka LLM tracing, PII redaction, fine-tuning, message-bus decoupling

1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

Most teams buy an observability tool and call it an architecture. The interesting part of this diagram isn't the vendor at the end, it's everything before it: a durable buffer so producers and consumers can fail independently, and PII redaction as its own service rather than a policy someone is supposed to follow. Compliance built into the pipeline is the difference between an AI system that runs in production and a demo legal shuts down. None of the hard parts here are AI parts.
