50k ft — Why care?

Artist Karyn (@frog_spit_simulation) used optical tweezers, a laser that traps tiny objects, to push neuroblast cells into words under a microscope. They then built layered 'skins' over the footage in ComfyUI from depth maps and diffusion passes. The talk is from DATLAB XIII, and the full presentation is on the YouTube livestream.
