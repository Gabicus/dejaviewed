50k ft — Why care?

The Elizabeth Greenshields Foundation gives CAD 17,000 first grants (20,000 on renewal) to early-career representational artists worldwide, with no fee, no CV and rolling applications by post. The Awesome Foundation's $1,000 monthly micro-grants (US, UK, Australia, Canada chapters) are the fallback for abstract or non-painting work.

1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

There’s a seventeen thousand dollar grant for painters just starting out. No CV, no deadline, no fee, and it’s open worldwide. It’s the Elizabeth Greenshields Foundation. Yesterday I mentioned a fund paying painters up to fifty thousand, and I was honest that it wants around ten years of exhibitions behind you. This is the other one. This one is built for the beginning. CAD 17,000 for a first grant. 20,000 if they fund you again. No application fee. And no deadline at all, they take applications right through the year from anywhere in the world. Last year they gave out close to 1.6 million between 93 artists. Two honest catches.
