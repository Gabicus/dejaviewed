50k ft — Why care?

The Elizabeth Greenshields Foundation gives CAD 17,000 first grants (20,000 on renewal) to early-career representational artists worldwide, with no fee, no CV and rolling applications by post. The Awesome Foundation's $1,000 monthly micro-grants (US, UK, Australia, Canada chapters) are the fallback for abstract or non-painting work.
arteducation

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Awesome Foundation chapters give $1,000/month with near-zero eligibility bar • Greenshields: representational work only (painting, drawing, sculpture, printmaking), application must be printed, signed and mailed • No deadline and open worldwide, roughly 93 artists funded last year
