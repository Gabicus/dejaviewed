50k ft — Why care?

A three-step oil glaze: once the painting is fully dry, mix your color with Galkyd Lite and Gamsol to a thin, transparent consistency (more Gamsol for thinner), then brush it over the target area.
art

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Galkyd Lite + Gamsol is the glazing medium; tune thinness with Gamsol • Only glaze over completely dry paint

5k ft — Tools & techniques

Galkyd Lite, Gamsol oil glazing

1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

First make sure the painting is dry before glazing Ok here’s how I did it! 1. I took my red color and put it in a container 2. I added galkyd Lite and gamsol together to create a thin layer (add more or less gamsol to get the consistency you desire) 3. Take your brush and apply over the deaired area! Enjoy! #oilpainting #surrealart #glazing #selfdiscovery #mystery #selfawareness #howto
