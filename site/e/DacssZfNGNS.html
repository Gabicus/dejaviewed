50k ft — Why care?

Ian Hubert shows physics-driven vehicle animation in Blender: active/passive rigid bodies, hinge and spring constraints, motor constraints pushing against the ground as thrusters, gravity off, and custom properties wired via drivers with scripted expressions for throttle and lights. Mentions Erindale's XInputReader addon for gamepad control.
3Danimationart

10k ft — Key takeaways

• A linear motor constrained to an immovable object acts like a thruster • Expose custom properties and drive them with scripted expressions for interactive control • Put thrust over the center of mass or the ship tumbles

5k ft — Tools & techniques

Blender, XInputReader drivers, motor constraints, rigid-body constraints, scripted expressions
