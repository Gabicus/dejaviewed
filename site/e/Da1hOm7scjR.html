50k ft — Why care?

Brain-dump your task list to Fable, have it write specs and spawn cheaper Opus subagents (in parallel worktrees) to execute, then Fable reviews and sends fixes back in a loop until it's good enough. You get top-model judgment without burning usage limits on implementation.
agentssoftware development

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Delegate implementation to cheaper subagents with written specs • Loop review and fix until the orchestrator approves • Use the expensive model for planning and review only

5k ft — Tools & techniques

Claude Code AI agents, orchestrator-worker pattern, review loop, subagent delegation

1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

We’re going to be in this loop of wanting to use the newest most expensive model without lighting dollars on fire for the rest of time so might as well get good at it
