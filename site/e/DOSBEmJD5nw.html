10k ft — Key takeaways

• Flexing snap tabs into holes can join plastic to cardboard without glue • Put a capture surface where ideas show up (the shower), not where you think they should • Review old raw sketches — early versions often already contain the final solution

1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

I’ve talked a little bit about this creative hack before but thought it was about time to give the full tour of the nitty and also gritty in the life of a shower notes taker. It’s not …. Glamorous. It’s not aesthetic. But it works. Also to all those people who think i don't shower.... receipts. Eat that.
