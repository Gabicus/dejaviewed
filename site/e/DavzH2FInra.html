1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

This project was inspired by the wind chimes we always had hanging in our doorway at home. In many cultures, they’re believed to protect the home and welcome good energy. I always loved the sound they made whenever someone walked through. Now that I live as a nomad, I think of every new country as opening a new door. And every door deserves its own chime. So I built this project just as a form of art and self-expression, where each country becomes a door inspired by its architecture, wisdom, and sounds. 👩🏼‍💻 Let me know if you want a tutorial on this one!
