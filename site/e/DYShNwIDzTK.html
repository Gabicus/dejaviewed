50k ft — Why care?

This is an opinionated privacy kit for going 'digitally off-grid'. It includes a self-built cyberdeck on a Rockchip-based Orange Pi, a Linux distro that doesn't ask for age verification, and GrapheneOS with the camera and accelerometer disabled. It adds a carrier that rotates your IMSI (likely Cape), a VPN you can pay for with mailed cash (likely Mullvad), and Tails on a USB stick for secure messaging. Treat the hardware-backdoor claims with skepticism.
