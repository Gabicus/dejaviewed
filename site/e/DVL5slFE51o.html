Visualizing the foundation of cross-asset modeling. 🌐📉 ​Safe assets like US Treasuries are the baseline for pricing risk across all markets. When macro shocks hit, the yield curve warps, and those ripples dictate cross-asset correlations. ​Watch how the 3D surface reacts to distinct macro regimes: ​The Volatility Shock (COVID-19): VIX explodes and yields violently compress as capital floods into safety. ​The Tightening Cycle: The Fed aggressively hikes, warping the surface and severely inverting the curve as the cost of short-term capital skyrockets. ​If your systemic models aren't dynamically adjusting to these structural shifts in the risk-free rate, your cross-asset correlations are likely breaking.
