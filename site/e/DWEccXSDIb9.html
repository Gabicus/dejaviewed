1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

The khaen is a traditional bamboo instrument from Laos and northeastern Thailand. It’s made of multiple bamboo pipes connected to a small wooden mouthpiece, and each pipe produces a different note. What makes it special is how you play it—you can both blow and breathe in to make sound. Inside the pipes are tiny metal reeds, so it actually works more like a harmonica than a flute. Because of this, players can create a continuous, flowing sound and even play simple chords. The sound is warm and slightly buzzing, often with a steady, hypnotic feel. Traditionally it’s used in folk music and ceremonies, but nowadays people also use it in modern styles like electronic or ambient music—like the guy you showed. 🎥@jerem.khaen
