50k ft — Why care?

This follow-up adds audio reactivity to the mood-board-to-code-art workflow. A well-defined design system lets AI agents generate generative visuals with parametric controls, including how audio drives the visuals. You end up with macros you can art-direct for live visuals.
agentsartaudiocreative codingdesign

10k ft — Key takeaways

• A tight design system makes agent output repeatable and tunable. • Break a mood board into a written design system the agent can follow. • Expose parameters as art-direction controls, including audio-to-visual mappings.
