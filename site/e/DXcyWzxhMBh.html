50k ft — Why care?

Chance McCoy plays Doc Watson's clawhammer banjo 'Shady Grove' in modal D-Dorian (pull-offs, drop-thumb at speed) and traces the minor melody to Jean Ritchie's father in Viper, Kentucky, and back to Child Ballad #81, 'Matty Groves'. A deep lineage lesson in one tune.

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Child Ballad #81 shows how stable folk melodies can stay over centuries • Learn Doc Watson's version for drop-thumb and pull-off technique in D-Dorian • The minor melody came via Jean Ritchie; trace sources before settling on a version
