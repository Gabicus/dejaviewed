Clawd plus MapQuest MCP just gave your AI actual location awareness. That means Clawd can understand what's nearby, how far apart places are, how long it'll take to get between them, and even factor in traffic. So here's how it works. Let's say I'm spending one day in New York. I can tell Clawd, find me somewhere for breakfast, somewhere to work, three things worth seeing, and somewhere for dinner. But instead of just giving me six random recommendations, Clawd can use MapQuest to find the actual places, calculate the routes between them, and put everything in the smartest order based on where I am right now. So breakfast could be a five-minute walk away. Then it finds the closest thing worth seeing to breakfast. Then it works out the fastest route across town and finishes the day somewhere that actually makes sense based on where I'll end up. What's cool is travel is just one example.
