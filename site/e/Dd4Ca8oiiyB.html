Transcript excerpt
Claude can now build an AI trading agent that watches the market and tells you exactly when something changes. Here's how it works. First, connect Claude to liquid trade, which lets it interact with stocks, crypto, forex, commodities, and prediction markets from inside Claude. Then connect QuiverQuant. This gives Claude market data, including things like insider transactions, politician trades, and institutional activity. Now you can give Claude a strategy. For example, tell it to watch a specific investor's disclosed trades and check for new activity throughout the day. Claude can compare that against your portfolio, analyze the new trade, and send you a recommendation when something changes. You can even create a routine so it keeps checking automatically without you having to ask. And before risking any actual money, you can run the entire thing through paper trading to see how the strategy performs. So you're basically giving Claude access to market data, a brokerage connection, and a set of rules to follow. Just comment trader and I'll send you the full setup.
