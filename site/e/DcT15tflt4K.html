50k ft — Why care?

StopSlop is a GitHub skill file that bans filler phrases, throat-clearing openers, emphasis crutches and adverbs so Claude's copy stops sounding like Claude. The creator finds it removes most tells but notes the deeper problem: AI prose lacks a position, so the last 20% still needs a human voice.

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Deleting tells doesn't add opinion; take a stance yourself. • Drop a banned-patterns skill into your writing workflow. • Use AI for the first 80%, edit the last 20% by hand.

1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

AI slop isn't a vocabulary problem, it's a stakes problem. Hedging, throat clearing, and adverb pileups are what writing looks like when nobody risks being wrong, and a model has no position it's afraid to defend. You can delete every tell on the list and the missing opinion survives the edit, which is why I think voice comes from what you're willing to claim, not from which words you cut.
