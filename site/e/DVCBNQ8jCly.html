How does a brainless colony find the shortest path through a maze without a map or a leader? The answer lies in a phenomenon called Stigmergy—a form of decentralized coordination where individuals interact through chemical modifications to their environment. This simulation brings Ant Colony Optimization (ACO) to life, modeling the precise feedback loops that govern swarm intelligence. Each autonomous agent manages two distinct data streams: a Home Trail and a Food Trail. As ants navigate between their nest and a resource, they deposit volatile pheromones. Because ants on a shorter path complete their round-trips faster, they reinforce their route with more pheromone per unit of time compared to those on longer detours.
