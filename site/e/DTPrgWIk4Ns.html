1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

Most traders set their Stop Loss based on "vibes" or static ATR. But what if the market itself has no memory? ​In this timelapse, I’m plotting the Hurst Exponent (H) on BTC-USD. This 3D surface shows how market efficiency changes across time and lookback windows. ​Why your SL is getting hit: ​When H < 0.5: The market is "Anti-persistent" (Mean-reverting). In this zone, "breakout" trades are SL-traps. The market is just noise. ​When H > 0.5: The market is "Persistent" (Trending). This is where your trend-following SLs actually work. ​When H = 0.5: It’s a Random Walk. You might as well flip a coin. 🪙 ​The Solution: Use the Hurst Surface to identify regime shifts.
