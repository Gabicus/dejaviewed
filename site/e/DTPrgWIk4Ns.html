50k ft — Why care?

Plots the Hurst Exponent of BTC-USD as a 3D surface. H>0.5 = trending (momentum), H<0.5 = mean-reverting, H=0.5 = random walk. Shows how market 'memory' varies across timeframes. Basis for adaptive strategy selection.

1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

Most traders set their Stop Loss based on "vibes" or static ATR. But what if the market itself has no memory? ​In this timelapse, I’m plotting the Hurst Exponent (H) on BTC-USD. This 3D surface shows how market efficiency changes across time and lookback windows. ​Why your SL is getting hit: ​When H < 0.5: The market is "Anti-persistent" (Mean-reverting). In this zone, "breakout" trades are SL-traps. The market is just noise. ​When H > 0.5: The market is "Persistent" (Trending). This is where your trend-following SLs actually work. ​When H = 0.5: It’s a Random Walk. You might as well flip a coin. 🪙 ​The Solution: Use the Hurst Surface to identify regime shifts. If the surface is dipping into the blue/purple (low H), widen your stops or stay out entirely. If it’s spiking into yellow/red,…
