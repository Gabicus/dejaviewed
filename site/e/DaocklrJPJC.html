50k ft — Why care?

Antidoom is Liquid AI's open-source pipeline that finds the exact token restarting a repetition loop, marks it rejected, picks up to 20 alternatives, and trains with Final Token Preference Optimization (FTPO) plus a KL-like regularizer in logit space so reasoning isn't damaged.
LLMopen sourcereferencevideo production

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Regularize in logit space to avoid wrecking general reasoning • Targeting the single loop-restart token fixes repetition surgically

5k ft — Tools & techniques

Antidoom, Claude Final Token Preference Optimization, KL regularization, preference tuning

1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

Liquid Al, the MIT spinout, just released Antidoom. It exists to fix one of Al’s biggest flaws! Antidoom targets the exact token score that triggers repetitive “doom loops” - the glitch that wastes context windows and ruins Al reasoning. It’s fully open source; and any developer can use it to unlock significantly more power. That said, using it requires serious hardware and deep Al knowledge.
