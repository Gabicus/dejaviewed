Today is August 12, 2026. Elon Musk’s SpaceXAI just launched Grok Bot, but the most interesting part isn’t simply that it can create AI workers. Each user gets a dedicated cloud computer shared across their Grok Bots. Those Bots can have different jobs, pass work between each other, create new long-lived Bots, and even run on different underlying models over time. That leads to a much bigger idea: your future “job” may not be one AI model or one agent. It could be a persistent cloud computer that accumulates tools, context, responsibilities, and increasingly capable AI workers. That’s my interpretation, not an xAI claim, but Grok Bot gives us a fascinating look at what that architecture could become. #Grok #ElonMusk #AI #FutureOfWork #gigaqian
