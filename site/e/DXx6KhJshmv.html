Transcript excerpt
What's the difference between context engineering, memory engineering, and harness engineering? That's a load of questions, but let me answer it very quickly. Context engineering is how you actually optimize the signals and noise ratio within the context window. So every information you put into the context window, you have to get every bank for your block. While memory engineering is how you actually implement systems that can learn. So, are you modeled the actual memory within the agents? Are you retrieving information? Are you forget information? Are you evolving information? Whereas harness engineering is everything around the agents and the LLM. So, it's the code, essentially. It's the scaffolding and the oprenulated structure of that scaffolding. That's harness engineering. Check out his course, by the way, on Deep Learning AI, all about agent memory. I'll link it below, and hello from New York again.
