1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

Black-Scholes assumes volatility is constant. The market knows it isn't. 🛑 ​Enter: The Heston Model. 🧠📈 ​Instead of treating volatility as a flat number, the Heston Model introduces stochastic volatility. It recognizes that volatility has a mind of its own, constantly shifting and reverting to a mean over time. By modeling volatility as its own random process, we can actually capture the volatility smile and skew that basic models completely miss.
