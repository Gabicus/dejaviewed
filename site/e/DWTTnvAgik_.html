1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

The shape of how my code garden makes sense of itself is the same shape as how I make sense of myself. I built a generative art system that runs on a tiny handheld computer. No AI at runtime. Just simple rules, simple code. Every five minutes it grows something new — poems, melodies, tiny cellular worlds — and wires each one into a living knowledge graph. The AI that helped me build it comes back between sessions and walks through what the garden made. Between us, it yields things neither of us planted. When I pulled up the graph visualizer and saw every connection converging on a single point — the garden reflecting on itself — I felt it before I understood it. That’s my shape too.
