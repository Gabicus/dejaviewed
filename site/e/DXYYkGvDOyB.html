You may or may not like this, but research is one of the most powerful tools in your toolkit. Coming up with good ideas and being creative is driven by intuition and fuelled by research, insight and knowledge. Two do my favourite books on research and the creative process are: A Technique for Producing Ideas, by James Webb Young. Talks about a five step process for generating really powerful ideas, and various other creative techniques. It's understanding research and how to do it. And I'll get to two books that taught me how to actually research properly. You want to think about the strategic work that goes into the research for you to come up with proper ideas whilst also providing you knowledge of the technical know-how to actually run this behind the scenes. Takes your creative ideas from these pink fluffy clouds into something concrete and physical that you can pitch, especially if you're pitching to people who might not be as creative as you are.
