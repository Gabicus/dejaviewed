Alright, so you were a quant at hedge funds helping them predict daily stock returns using machine learning. Yes, so it's called finding alphas. Finding alphas, and how accurate did it get? We can reach around 5% to 10%. How do you calculate that? We use historical stock data to get a prediction on stock returns on the next day, and then compare it with actual next-day returns. This algorithm to use historical to predict the next days is called finding alphas. Our relation is calculated by multiplying the predicted returns and actual returns, and summing up for all stocks. To get that 5% to 10%, and if you constantly can predict that, they pay you $100 to $200,000 for the algorithm. Yes.
