we need to talk about what's going on with CoreWeave. It plunged 15% and is taking a bunch of the other AI stocks down with it. I happened to work in CDO structuring at Morgan Stanley in 2008. I'm going to explain exactly what's going on in under three minutes. Let's begin with where we are within the AI ecosystem. We're in layer three, the infrastructure, which at its core can be thought of as the companies who sell compute. Now compute is the raw processing power needed to train AI models. And Wall Street splits those companies into two categories. First there are the hyperscalers, household names like Amazon's AWS or Microsoft's Azure. And second is the neoclouds, where the poster children are CoreWeave and Nebius. To understand the neocloud business, let's run through what NVIDIA CEO Jensen Wong calls an AI factory. First you need energy, second you need chips, and third you need the building or powered shell. Many of these neoclouds started as crypto miners, but pivoted their business when they realized AI was a better opportunity. The output of the AI factory is compute, but companies make money in different ways. Applied digital and core scientific are landlords. They own the powered shells and make money renting the real estate without taking on the risk of buying the chips.
