The market is 90% noise and 10% signal. 📉 ​Standard correlation matrices fail because they treat random market fluctuations as valid connections. By applying Random Matrix Theory (RMT), we can "clean" the matrix—suppressing the noise eigenvalues and isolating the dominant market modes. ​This isn't magic. It's linear algebra.
