1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

Striking distance keywords - how to rank high for them. Go to Google Search Console. This is Google’s own tool for submitting your website to Google’s index. If you haven’t submitted your site to it, do it. Next, go to Search Results. Toggle on Average Position. Look for keywords ranking between positions 6 and 11. These are striking distance keywords, meaning they are ranking near the bottom of Google’s first page or the top of its second. Click a keyword that looks interesting. See the page that is ranking for it. All you have to do to improve your ranking for this keyword is add a few extra sentences to your page about it.
