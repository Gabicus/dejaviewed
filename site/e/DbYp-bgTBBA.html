Charlie Munger's inversion — solve problems by asking how to cause failure

50k ft — Why care?

Munger explains inversion with his WWII meteorologist job: ask how you'd kill pilots (icing, running out of fuel), then avoid those. Same move for any problem: list what would hurt it most and steer clear.
finance/tradingproductivity

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Pairs naturally with pre-mortems • Reframe 'how do I succeed' as 'what would guarantee failure' and avoid those paths • Works for system design and risk reviews as well as investing

Charlie Munger (January 1, 1924 – November 28, 2023) was an American investor, businessman, attorney, and philanthropist best known as the longtime vice chairman of Berkshire Hathaway. Widely regarded as one of the greatest business thinkers of his generation, Munger played a pivotal role in shaping Berkshire Hathaway alongside Warren Buffett. His multidisciplinary approach to decision-making and emphasis on rational thinking transformed the way countless investors and entrepreneurs approach business and life. Originally trained as a lawyer, Munger developed an investment philosophy that extended beyond traditional financial analysis.
