1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

Traditional portfolios hold directional asset beta. Quantitative systems trade statistical edge and implied volatility discrepancies. When execution algorithms outpace market microstructure latency compounding capital becomes an invariant math outcome. This is how math become a money printer. Renaissance exploits short horizon mean reversion and non linear statistical arbitrage. By mapping high dimensional phase spaces and identifying non random hidden Markov states their models extracted a gross sixty six percent annualized return. Citadel utilizes a multi strategy pod architecture that integrates systematic statistical arbitrage alongside fundamental execution layers to yield the fifty three percent cumulative return shown in the chart.
