Transcript excerpt
Here's how you can build these kind of scroll animations that agencies pay thousands for. In four steps with zero code. You know the ones where you scroll and the product spins or builds itself on screen. Here's how to make it. Step one, go to Google Flow and generate two images with these prompts. So basically one is for the start frame and another for the end frame. Step two, in Flow, pick frames to video, upload both images and let it animate everything in between. Step three, take that clip to Easy GIF, use video to image sequence, set it to 30 frames per second and download all the frames as a zip. Step four, drop that whole folder into your AI coding tool and tell it to build a scroll linked image sequence on a canvas. So the animation plays frame by frame as you scroll. And by the way, this is the exact trick that Apple uses for their product pages. Come and prompt and I'll send you the full walkthrough.
