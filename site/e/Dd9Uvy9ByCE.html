50k ft — Why care?

Michael Fogleman's ln (Go) renders 3D scenes as 2D vector paths by ray-testing each path segment for visibility, supporting CSG, math surfaces and procedural cities. vpype then merges and sorts paths for plotting, cutting pen-up travel 86% in this example. Both MIT licensed, output straight to SVG.

1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

ln + vpype — turning 3D scenes into lines for drawing. ① Build a 3D scene → ② remove hidden lines → ③ export vector paths → ④ arrange the drawing order. ln removes lines hidden by the geometry. vpype joins and reorders the paths for pen plotting. These scenes use ln's original examples with added camera motion. In this example, vpype linemerge + linesort reduced pen-up travel by 86% (105,271 → 14,926 px). This measures distance, not the time of a real pen plotter. Which scene would you turn into a print, an animation, or a pen plot?
