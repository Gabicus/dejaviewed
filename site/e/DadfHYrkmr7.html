"Clipping," the practice of cutting up a creator's content, adding captions, and reposting it across social media, is quietly becoming a massive industry. Brands are now paying armies of everyday people to flood social feeds with their content. CNN's @bijanhosseini dives into the phenomenon that's changing advertising and creating Gen Z millionaires.
