Most founders are prompting Claude wrong. They tell it to build the website. That’s the mistake. Claude isn’t a designer — it’s a translator. It converts decisions into pixels. The real prompt isn’t «build me a premium website.» The real prompt is «what should the visitor feel in the three seconds before they read anything.» Writing that sentence took 5 years in branding. Building the site took 6 hours. Nobody is telling you this part. Claude doesn’t hand you taste. It accelerates the taste you brought to the table.
