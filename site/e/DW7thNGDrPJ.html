50k ft — Why care?

Final part of a Kronos explainer: after binary spherical quantization and hierarchical encoding turn candles into tokens, a transformer uses self-attention (query/key/value per candle) to weight relevant bars like a volume spike over chop, and an autoregressive decoder predicts the next tokens, i.e. future price action. Contrasts with sequential LSTMs.
LLMcreative codingfinance/tradingquantvideo production

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Attention lets the model weight distant but relevant bars. • Open model you can test on your own markets. • Tokenize OHLCV candles, then forecast with an autoregressive transformer.

5k ft — Tools & techniques

Kronos autoregressive decoding, binary spherical quantization, self-attention
