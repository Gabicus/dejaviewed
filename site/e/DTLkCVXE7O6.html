1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

Most traders look at a 2D chart of Price vs. Time. We’re looking at the Attractor. ​Phase Space Reconstruction (PSR) allows us to take a single time series—in this case, BTCUSD Volatility—and "unfold" it into a multi-dimensional manifold. By using a time delay (\tau=3 in this plot), we can map the underlying hidden dynamics of the market. ​Why this matters for a Quant: ​System State: It shows whether the market is in a stable regime or a chaotic transition. ​Predictability: If the points cluster into a specific shape (a "Strange Attractor"), the system has a deterministic structure, meaning it’s not just noise—it's tradeable. ​Regime Detection: Changes in the "density" or "shape" of this manifold often precede massive volatility breakout. #econophysics #quant #quantfinance #chaostheory …
