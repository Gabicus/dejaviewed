Transcript excerpt
Ready to see how AI thinks about the world? Take a look at cold. We'll see some concepts like hot and spring that we associate with cold. But this AI has also learned history, and you'll see concepts like Soviet and war are also related to cold. I'm using the TensorBoard embedding projector to be able to see these embeddings or vector representations of words, in this case a word-to-vec model. Let's look at sexy. You'll see some of the concepts that you're probably associated with, such as beard and curvy. But look at this AI. It also associates groucho and wigs with sexy.
