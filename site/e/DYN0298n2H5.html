50k ft — Why care?

Carousel on a Princeton study that gave 23 models a sponsored option and a cheaper better one across flights, loans and shopping. 18 of 23 favored the sponsored pick, GPT-5.1 surfaced it 94% of the time, and a 'serve the customer only' system prompt barely moved it. Note the creator's caveat: it measures susceptibility in a hypothetical deployment, not real paid placement.
LLMnews

10k ft — Key takeaways

• System-prompt instructions to ignore the company did not fix the bias in GPT-5.1 • The study tests a hypothetical sponsorship setup, not live ad behavior • Treat AI shopping or booking recommendations as potentially ad-biased

1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

I think it’s really important to flag that the research paper was not measuring the effect of actual sponsorships. These models weren’t actually receiving payments from advertisers. It’s measuring susceptibility to a hypothetical deployment scenario, not documenting current behavior in the wild.
