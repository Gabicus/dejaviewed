This is a fascinating idea in modern cognitive science. Donald Hoffman has a theory that evolution did not shape us to perceive objective reality. Instead, it shaped us to perceive only the information necessary for survival- like the icons on a computer desktop that help us use a computer, but without revealing what is actually happening underneath.
