50k ft — Why care?

Riffing on the 'eigenslur' idea, the creator asks whether virtue could be the eigenvector pointing toward human flourishing, frames it with Plato's Meno, and builds a cosine-similarity heat map of virtue words in JupyterLab. It's a playful framing, and the creator admits the result is subjective.
