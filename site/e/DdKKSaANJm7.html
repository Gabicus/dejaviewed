50k ft — Why care?

Noma Labs research shows AI workflows (email auto-responders, GitHub Actions, Google email flows) leak data when an attacker simply asks, because nothing checks whether the requester is authorized. Not prompt injection, the model is doing what it's told. The fix is bringing identity and authorization into agent workflows.
agentsopen sourceprompt engineeringsecurity

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Audit email and CI automations that act on inbound messages • Check requester identity before an AI workflow returns data • Distinguish this from direct and indirect prompt injection • link: https://vulnu.xyz/noma-ig
