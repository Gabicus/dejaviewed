Transcript excerpt
Most vibe-coded apps today look like AI slob. Here's a six-step process to avoid that. First, build the ugly prototype in Google AI Studio with zero prompts for aesthetics. Only once you get the logic and the functionality right, you start defining the brand's soul. For this, prompt Claude for something specific like calm and analog to stay away from the usual tech slob. Next, move to this Pinterest alternative to pick five to 10 images that capture your taste and feed them to Webii AI. By the way, I recommend using Flux 2 Pro to build a cohesive palette, then create a visual anchor, like say a vintage cassette, and let that reference guide the elements of the app. Now, for the most important part, the logo, use Idogram V3 and prompt it with strong negative statements like these. Finally, assemble everything in Figma and feed these screenshots back into Google AI Studio to vibe code toward your Northstar design.
