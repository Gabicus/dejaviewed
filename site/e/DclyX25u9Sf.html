1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

Four Italian engineers watched their first startup die and started over with $40,000. That $40,000 became an $18 billion public offering. Not by building something new. By buying what everyone else had already written off. Bending Spoons identified stagnated platforms that were once worth hundreds of millions. Evernote. Vimeo. Meetup. They acquired them for pennies on the dollar, sent in a lean team of elite engineers, rebuilt only the core functionality customers actually used, and let the margin expansion compound into the next acquisition. The margin expansion was not a bonus. It was the strategy. AI native acquirers are not arriving as vendors pitching a better tool.
