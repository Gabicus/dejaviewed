Why would you subscribe to Clawd only to use 10% of its brain? Here's a setup that makes it worth the buy. Start by creating the agents.md file. It's what you would first give an employee while onboarding them. Your business, voice, and how you like to work. Clawd references that every time before it does anything. Next is the context folder. This contains heavier and more nuanced information, but feeding context alone isn't enough. It also needs to remember your preferences. Say you ask it to stop signing emails with cheers, it logs that in the memory.md file, which it continuously updates as it learns about you. And lastly, create the skills folder where you literally walk through a process once and it packages all of it into a reusable workflow. That's how you can turn a four-hour task into a single instruction. Comment, stack, and I'll deem you the detailed setup.
