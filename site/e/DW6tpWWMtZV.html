50k ft — Why care?

An interactive piece by @pepepepebrick: MediaPipe hand tracking moves two digital spiders across an algorithmically generated 3D web in TouchDesigner, with VCV Rack making the sound. Legs grow near nodes and leg count drives pitch and effects, spider distance changes the waveform, and contact fuses the bodies and shifts color and web structure.
3Dartaudiocreative coding

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Credits point to @blankensmithing's MediaPipe tooling for TouchDesigner • Map game-like state such as leg count and distance to synth parameters in VCV Rack • MediaPipe hand tracking in TouchDesigner drives both visuals and audio

5k ft — Tools & techniques

MediaPipe, TouchDesigner, VCV Rack generative geometry, hand tracking, parameter mapping to sound

1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

🧱 2026/04/09 • What if Spiders Wove with Strings?🕷️ 【如果蜘蛛可以用弦結網？】 • This is a hand-tracking audio and visual interactive system where users control digital spiders with their hands. At the center of the visual is a bio-inspired, algorithmically generated 3D web. As a spider’s body approaches the nodes, legs emerge and grow, where the number of legs modulates the pitch and effects—as if they were standing on musical strings. • The two spiders can interact, flirt, or engage in courtship, with their proximity altering the sound’s waveform.
