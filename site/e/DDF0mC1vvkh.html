Interacting with a 3D world generated by a picture or a sentence is now a reality. A company called World Labs just released their first demo, which allows you to generate consistent 3D worlds from a single 2D image 🤯 To me this would be already be a cool Quest / Vision Pro app. Being able to describe a world in detail and then stepping into them to explore. Some artists are already using this tech to fill certain gaps in the creative workflow. For example, an artist named Eric Solorio says it makes it easier to stage characters within scenes and direct precise camera movements. As part of the demo, you can also experience different camera effects, 3D effects, and dive into classic paintings.
