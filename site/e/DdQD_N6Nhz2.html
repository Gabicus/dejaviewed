Expired-domain rebuild — a black-hat SEO backlink tactic
techniqueB-tier

50k ft — Why care?

Buy a relevant expired domain, rebuild it from the Wayback Machine, force-index with IndexChex, prop it up with cheap niche edits, then add one contextual link to your site once it ranks and push paid traffic through it. Requires footprint hiding (separate emails, hosting, IPs). Explicitly black-hat with penalty risk.
marketing

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Black-hat, so risk of manual penalties • Google links related domains via registration and hosting footprints • Inherited domain authority is the lever

5k ft — Tools & techniques

ExpiredDomains.net, IndexChex, Wayback Machine expired domain rebuild, niche edits

1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

This is pretty devious. Go buy a domain from Expired Domains. Check on Wayback Machine. Make sure the site is relevant to your website. Rebuild the expired domain you just bought. That includes adding the content. So you’re making this expired domain you just bought match what you see on Wayback Machine. Force index the rebuilt expired domain using IndexChex. Send cheap niche edits to it. You can just build easy backlinks to this site. Once this starts ranking for your keywords, then and only then, add a homepage link to your site. Make it natural plus contextually relevant. Now run traffic through that backlink from whatever source you like, Facebook ads, Reddit ads, whatever you want. See what it does to your site.
