50k ft — Why care?

A study of 31 open-weight models on 1,500 problems found small models beat much larger ones on ~8% of problems, but forcing the big models to be concise reversed it. Ties into the caveman repo that makes Claude Code talk tersely; the claim is brevity improves accuracy, not just token cost, possibly undoing RLHF-learned over-explaining.
LLMagentsopen sourceprompt engineeringsoftware development

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Caveman-style output rules also cut tokens. • Instructing concise answers can raise accuracy on large models. • Verbose reasoning can talk a model into wrong answers.
