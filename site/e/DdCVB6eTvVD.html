Title Identity (2025) Summary Identity is a fast-paced Malayalam action-thriller that revolves around a high-stakes investigation following a high-profile crime. When the sole eyewitness—a woman with a photographic memory—claims she can identify the killer, a skilled sketch artist and a dedicated law enforcement officer team up to unravel the mystery. As they dig deeper, hidden secrets and dark twists emerge, blurring the lines between memory, identity, and justice.
