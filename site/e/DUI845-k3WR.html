The mathematical backbone of Mean Reversion strategies. 📉📈 Visualizing the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck (OU) process. Unlike standard Brownian motion (which models stock prices), the OU process models things that oscillate around a stable value—like volatility, interest rates, or the spread between two correlated assets in Pairs Trading.
