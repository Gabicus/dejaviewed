50k ft — Why care?

Open-source, browser-based Gaussian splat manipulator: load 3DGS scans and add audio reactivity, depth of field, lens curvature, swirly bokeh, film emulation, quantisation and render export, no plugins or TouchDesigner needed. Built with World Labs' SparkJS and help from Claude Code; works on desktop and phones.

1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

Gausseous: Open source, free to use browser based, audio-reactive gaussian splat manipulator. Depth of field, lens curvature, swirly bokeh, film emulation, quantisation, render export - all browser based, no plugins. Best on desktop but works on phones. I made this in response to students asking how to play with 3DGS scans to create experimental visuals without paying for plugins or loosing hours to the Touch Designer time sink, but it has grown into something more than a basic intro into playing with splats.
