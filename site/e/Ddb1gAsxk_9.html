100k ft — What is this?

Fish Audio phone agent — AI receptionist that gathers context then hands off
techniqueA-tier

50k ft — Why care?

A support line where a Fish Audio voice agent on a Twilio number discloses it is AI, asks consent to take notes, collects context into database tables, then calls you, briefs you and merges the call. Repeat callers get their history recalled. The agent never tries to resolve issues itself, avoiding Taco Bell-style failures.
agentsaudioprompt engineeringvideo production

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Brief the human then merge calls to cut resolution time • Disclose AI and ask consent for notes upfront • Persist caller context for repeat calls • Use the voice agent for intake and context, not resolution

5k ft — Tools & techniques

Fish Audio, Twilio AI agents, caller context memory, human handoff, workflow automation
