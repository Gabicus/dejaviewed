Transcript excerpt
I built an autonomous pen testing agent on a OnePlus 8 with Callie Net Hunger. It runs a local TB model LLM on the GPU, fully air-gaped, no cloud accessible. It rotates hose, it treats itself like a patient adversary to stay on the network, trying little things here and there while covering itself with cover traffic. So it will go to Instagram sometimes, and other times it will actually start knocking on ports. It also has a Wi-Fi breaching pipeline, so if you don't have the Wi-Fi password in the network that you're trying to address, you can just plug in a Wi-Fi dongle and then they'll figure it out. And I'm using it just to harden my own infrastructure. Everything is audit logged, every command goes through a proxy, and it has a bunch of CVs preloaded on it, so it knows what it's looking for. And in the end, it's a really cool red tumor that just fits in your pocket, all open source. Link coming out later.
