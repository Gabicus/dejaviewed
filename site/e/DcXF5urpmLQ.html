50k ft — Why care?

Moderna and Merck reported the first positive Phase 3 result for an individualized neoantigen mRNA therapy in 1,137 resected high-risk melanoma patients: sequence tumor vs. normal DNA, algorithmically pick up to 34 neoantigens, encode them in patient-specific mRNA, and combine with Keytruda. Overall survival still being tracked; not yet approved.
