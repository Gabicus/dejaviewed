50k ft — Why care?

Researchers queried four American LLMs via API without system prompts, in Chinese, about their identity. GPT and Gemini answered correctly, but Claude Opus 4.8 claimed to be Chinese (mostly Qwen) 60% of the time and Grok 97% (mostly DeepSeek), a likely trace of distillation-style synthetic training data.
