Is Samurai Jack the most aesthetic cartoon ever? This series is an absolute work of art from start to finish. Often presenting its story through aesthetic visuals rather than traditional exposition, the show’s artist drew inspiration primarily from traditional art forms rather than the standard cartoonish style of animation. Part of this is the application of the 60:30:10 color rule, a golden standard in design and art which breaks the frame into a dominant color (60%), a secondary color (30%) and an accent color (10%).
