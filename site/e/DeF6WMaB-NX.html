50k ft — Why care?

Bruce Knuteson (ex-MIT physicist, D. E. Shaw) splits each day into close-to-open and open-to-close: Micron since 1990 is +138M% overnight and -99.9% intraday, and the same sawtooth shows in 21 indices (China reversed). His paper 'Strikingly Suspicious Overnight and Intraday Returns' rejects earnings and news as explanations and alleges large firms push prices at the open; it's a claim, not a proof.
finance/tradingquant

10k ft — Key takeaways

• Decompose returns into overnight and intraday legs; the two compound to buy-and-hold • Paper: Knuteson, 'Strikingly Suspicious Overnight and Intraday Returns' (arXiv, 2020) • Pattern holds across 21 global indices, so it's not a single-stock artifact • Trading costs and gaps at the open matter before treating this as a strategy
