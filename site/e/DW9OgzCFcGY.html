1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

A simple point about extremism In this 1987 talk, John Cleese breaks down how extreme thinking turns disagreement into division; by simplifying issues, reinforcing “us vs. them,” and creating unnecessary enemies. His message is straightforward: nuance, openness, and critical thinking are the antidotes. Fun fact: Cleese wrote and performed this piece as a satirical but serious critique of how easily people are pulled toward rigid, polarized thinking. Big explain, the great thing about having enemies is that you can pretend that all the badness in the whole world is in your enemies and all the goodness in the whole world is in you.
