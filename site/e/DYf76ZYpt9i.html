50k ft — Why care?

NYU's Center for Quantum Information Physics, Qunnect and Cisco ran a three-node hub-and-spoke quantum network over ordinary commercial fiber between Brooklyn and Lower Manhattan. Entanglement swapping happened at the hub, the endpoints ran at room temperature, and automatic polarization controllers made up for vibration and temperature drift. It's an explainer for a real research result.
