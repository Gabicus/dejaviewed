50k ft — Why care?

Anna Zhang's browser camera filters out every pixel that stays the same between frames, leaving traces of motion like a butterfly leaving, rain hitting pavement, branches swaying. A creative-coding piece about slowing down attention, and you can try it live at change-capture.anna-zhang.com.

Transcript excerpt
I made Change Capture, a camera that only captures what changes. It ignores everything still, so the image becomes less about what a place looks like and more about what happened there. A butterfly flying away, raindrops falling onto the ground, a tree swaying in the wind. The tiny things you only notice when you stay with the scene for a little longer. Interested in cameras not just as tools for documenting, but as tools for changing how we pay attention. Change Capture slows the act of taking a photo down. Instead of instantly grabbing a perfect image, I wanted to create something that would make you wait and watch, and notice what the world is doing in between moments.
