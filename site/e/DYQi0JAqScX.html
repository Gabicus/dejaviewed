50k ft — Why care?

Anna Zhang's browser camera filters out every pixel that stays the same between frames, leaving traces of motion like a butterfly leaving, rain hitting pavement, branches swaying. A creative-coding piece about slowing down attention, and you can try it live at change-capture.anna-zhang.com.

1k ft — Deep-dive / try it

creative coding to rethink what a camera can be… beyond a device that records a scene literally but one that changes the way you notice what’s happening around you 📹 Change Capture filters out anything that stays the same between frames. What’s left is not a traditional photograph, but a trace of movement and time. A butterfly leaving the frame, rain hitting the pavement, branches swaying in the wind… small moments that usually disappear into the background become the subject. I wanted to make a camera that resists the quick snapshot. One that asks you to slow down, stay with what’s in front of you, and notice the tiny changes unfolding in your environment.
