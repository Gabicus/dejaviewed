Thesis

Across the catalog, the systems you interact with are quietly optimizing against you: a puzzle game runs an on-device XGBoost model to time difficulty to your ad value, overnight and intraday returns run as a decades-long sawtooth, a bedroom spoofer tripped depth-reading algorithms in the 2010 Flash Crash, wash trading inflates crypto volume, and exchanges sold speed to HFTs. Agent-plus-Ghidra decompilation and cheap tick data mean one person can now audit the counterparty instead of guessing about it.

Why it matters

This is game theory made practical: before you look for edge, measure the edge that's already being taken from you — your fills, the hours you hold, the apps you pay. It's defensive leverage, and it often turns into an offensive idea, like simply holding overnight.

Action sketch — how to implement this

Split two years of SPY and QQQ returns into close-to-open and open-to-close legs, then subtract realistic open-gap spreads to see whether the overnight leg survives costs.

Export your own broker fills and compare each to the NBBO at the time, so you measure the price improvement or slippage you actually got.

For one paid or free-to-play app you use, run REA MCP on your own install and list every model or rule that adapts to you (authorized analysis only).

falsifier: if the overnight leg disappears after costs and your fills match or beat the NBBO, the counterparty isn't taking much from you at your size — move your effort to the strategy-loop dive.
