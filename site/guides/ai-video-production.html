The One-Person Video Studio: AI Tools That Replace a Production Team

Insight class: Workflow Multiplier · Difficulty: Easy · Quality: 4/5 Connected entries: 7 · Tools: ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, DALL-E, GPT-4, Kling, Midjourney, OpenAI, OpenScreen, Premiere Pro, Runway, Runway Gen-3, Sora, Veo, siddharthvaddem/openscreen

Thesis

Runway + Kling + rotoscoping + motion brush = professional video production without a crew, studio, or actors. Direct scenes with text prompts and motion controls.

Why it matters

Professional video production has three bottlenecks: talent (actors/crew), equipment (cameras/lighting/studio), and post-production (editing/VFX). AI tools collapse all three. Runway's node canvas replaces the editing suite. Kling's motion brush replaces the director ('move this character's arm here'). Rotoscoping lets you insert yourself into any scene without a green screen. OpenScreen handles screen recording for tutorials. The result: one person with a laptop produces content that previously needed a production team. The cost dropped from $10K/video to $30/month in subscriptions.
