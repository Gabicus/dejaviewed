The Self-Improving AI Workspace: Claude Code That Gets Smarter Every Session

Insight class: Life Leverage · Difficulty: Medium · Quality: 5/5 Connected entries: 6 · Tools: Anthropic, Claude, Claude Code, Obsidian

Thesis

CLAUDE.md + primer.md + memory hooks + Obsidian + parallel worktrees = an AI dev environment that learns your preferences, remembers context, and runs multiple streams simultaneously.

Why it matters

Most people use Claude as a chatbot — ask a question, get an answer, context dies. This stack makes Claude a persistent collaborator that remembers your preferences, coding style, and project history across sessions. CLAUDE.md is the living rulebook (it self-improves when you correct mistakes), primer.md carries session state across context resets, parallel worktrees let you run 10-15 Claude sessions simultaneously on different parts of the same codebase. The compounding effect is real: after a week, Claude knows your patterns better than a new hire would after a month. After a month, it's like having a senior engineer who's read every line of your code.
