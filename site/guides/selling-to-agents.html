Selling to Agents — metered data for buyers that don't have eyes

Insight class: Arbitrage Opportunity · Difficulty: Medium · Quality: 3/5 Connected entries: 9 · Tools: Agent Reach, Anthropic, Binance, ChatGPT, Claude, Claude Code, Google AI Overview, Grok, LinkedIn, OpenAI, Salesforce, USYC, oc

Thesis

Three saves describe one gap. Quality publishers block AI crawlers, so AI search cites whatever is left. AI assistants favor the sponsored option in 18 of 23 models, so they are steerable buyers. Software is going headless, so agents pick tools by speed, cost and accuracy instead of interface. x402 revives HTTP 402 so an agent can pay a stablecoin micropayment per request. A one-person owner of a clean niche dataset can sit exactly in that gap — machine-legible, crawler-reachable, and metered.

Why it matters

This is distribution without an audience. The buyer is an agent that never sees your landing page, so a solo builder with good data competes on structure and price, not on ads. DejaViewed's own catalog and API are the obvious first item to sell.

Stablecoin plumbing under the GENIUS Act — the rail is becoming bank-grade

Under the GENIUS Act banks can issue stablecoins, park the backing cash in short-term Treasury bills, keep the roughly 4% yield (the law bars passing it to holders), and repo the T-bills for liquidity. Action sketch — how to implement this

Expose one niche dataset you own (start with the DejaViewed catalog's api/ JSON) as a free llms.txt summary plus a richer x402-metered endpoint with structured, citation-ready records.

Wrap the same endpoint as an MCP server and list it publicly so Claude and other agents can find it; log every request, free and paid, with user agent.

Run a 60-day A/B on price per call (free, 0.1c, 1c) and track how often your records show up in Perplexity and ChatGPT answers.

falsifier: if after 60 days you see plenty of free crawls but close to zero paid calls, agents are not buying yet. The demand side doesn't exist and this is a 2027 idea, not a 2026 one.
