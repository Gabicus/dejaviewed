The Album Is a Device Again — Offline Generative Music Objects

Tristan Perich already shipped a symphony as a chip in a CD case.
What a pro adds: A generative composer in the Brian Eno / Tristan Perich line would design the variation space before any player code: a stem compatibility matrix…
Proof it worked: Twenty or more blind listeners: an ABX and preference test against the studio master, scripted shuffle, results in listening.csv with a binomial confidence interval.
Biggest trap: Inaudible variation. Fix: require a minimum number of changed stems between plays and vary section order.
