Autonomous Agents in the Wild: What's Actually Working in 2026

Autonomous agents went from demo-ware to daily drivers in 2026. But the gap between 'impressive demo' and 'reliable tool' is massive.
What a pro adds: A builder who ships reliable agents, not demos, follows the pattern these entries converge on: narrow scope, fast feedback, cheap failure.
Proof it worked: The agent completes its narrow task unattended across a full cycle without exceeding budget or scope, per a complete append-only audit log.
Biggest trap: Scope creep: 'narrow' agent given many tools and open goals. Fix: renarrow to one task, 1-2 tools, cheap failure.
