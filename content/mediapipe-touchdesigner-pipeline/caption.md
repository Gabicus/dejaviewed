MediaPipe × TouchDesigner: Real-Time Body-to-Art Pipeline

MediaPipe's body/hand/face tracking combined with TouchDesigner's node-based visual programming creates a real-time pipeline where human movement becomes generative art.
What a pro adds: A working interactive-installation artist does not wire a demo and stop.
Proof it worked: The .toe opens and runs from the committed repo on a second machine (or clean profile) with a fresh TouchDesigner+Python install, no manual rewiring.
Biggest trap: Jittery output: raw landmarks drive parameters with no filter. Fix: one-euro filter plus dead-zones per channel.
