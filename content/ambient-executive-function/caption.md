Ambient Executive Function — glanceable hardware for agents and attention

OpenAI built a $230 hardware controller for babysitting coding agents. Someone answered with a MacBook-notch status app, and Bot Crossing turned 80 agents into characters who raise their hands.
What a pro adds: A human-factors researcher would design the 14 days as an ABAB single-case study, not a before/after: baseline logging with the display off, display on, phone notifications instead, display on again.
Proof it worked: Event logs: analysis.py on the SQLite export gives per-phase wait time and glance-triggered actions with bootstrap intervals…
Biggest trap: Stuck count. Fix: clear on PreToolUse and Stop, expire after 2 hours.
