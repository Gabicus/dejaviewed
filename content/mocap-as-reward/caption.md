Mocap as Reward — teaching cheap robots to move like you

The Microduck RL demo shows the classic failure: a loose reward function makes the robot 'stand' in a way nobody intended. The catalog also holds the fix, scattered across collections.
What a pro adds: A legged-robotics researcher would recognize this as the DeepMimic and Adversarial Motion Priors (AMP) problem and follow the closest real precedent: Disney Research's bipedal character work…
Proof it worked: The repo's own baseline: the reward hack reproduced from its default config as the reference failure.
Biggest trap: Infeasible reference. Fix: slow and shrink the clip, or switch to AMP.
