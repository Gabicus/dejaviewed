Anything With Geometry — Ink on Paper: the Pen-Plotter Loop

Fogleman's ln turns any 3D scene into hidden-line vector paths, and vpype makes those paths plotter-ready.
What a pro adds: A working plotter artist would treat line density as the real design variable: they would measure millimetres of ink per square centimetre per region…
Proof it worked: The pen plotter (AxiDraw via axicli or a GRBL machine via vpype-gcode) completes each A3 plot without hand-edited SVG, paper tear or bleed-through…
Biggest trap: Triangle mush: rips and scans render every triangle edge. Fix: FeatureMesh with welding and a dihedral threshold, slice hatching for flat areas.
