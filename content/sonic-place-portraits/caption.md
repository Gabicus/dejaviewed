Capture the Room Twice — Splat Scans and Song for Roots Music

Roots musicians already treat the room as an instrument — testing a 200-year-old house's resonance, filming tunes on porches and in old venues, the way Lomax filmed singers in their own kitchens.
What a pro adds: A location-recording engineer and a volumetric-capture artist would treat the room as data captured once, correctly.
Proof it worked: The exported .splat opens and reacts in Gausseous in a desktop browser at an acceptable frame rate, with no holes in the performance area.
Biggest trap: Noisy low-light scan. Fix: rescan with even light and overlapping coverage, or fall back to DIY LiDAR for big dark rooms.
