Offline AI Appliances — one job, one box, no cloud

Gemma Translator puts speech recognition, translation and TTS on a Raspberry Pi 5, and it ships with a bill of materials and a printable case.
What a pro adds: An embedded product engineer would set a latency and accuracy budget per stage (capture, OCR, summarize, TTS) and measure each on the target board under sustained thermal load…
Proof it worked: A tcpdump capture across the full 20-letter run with zero non-loopback packets.
Biggest trap: Invented amounts: the summarizer fills in a plausible due date. Fix: verbatim check that forces 'not sure' when a value is missing from the OCR.
