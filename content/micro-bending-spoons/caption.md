Buying Dead Apps — the one-person AI-native rollup

What a pro adds: A search-fund operator or micro-PE buyer would verify revenue from the payment processor and bank, never from seller screenshots, and would look at cohort churn…
Proof it worked: Read-only processor exports and bank statements reconciled to the listing within 5%.
Biggest trap: Screenshot revenue: seller dashboards overstate. Fix: require processor read-only access and bank statements before shortlisting.
