The One-Person Video Studio: AI Tools That Replace a Production Team

Runway + Kling + rotoscoping + motion brush = professional video production without a crew, studio, or actors. Direct scenes with text prompts and motion controls.
What a pro adds: A producer would start with a script, a shot list and a continuity bible (character reference sheet, wardrobe, palette, lens and lighting per scene) before generating a frame…
Proof it worked: ffprobe shows target resolution, constant frame rate and codec; ffmpeg loudnorm reports integrated loudness between -15 and -13 LUFS.
Biggest trap: Character drift. Fix: generate start frames from one reference sheet with fixed style references and seeds.
