Body → Machine → Art: Real-Time Interactive Installations Anyone Can Build

Kinect/MediaPipe body tracking + TouchDesigner + Unreal Engine = interactive art installations that respond to human movement. Previously needed a studio budget; now runs on a gaming laptop.
What a pro adds: A creative technologist would start from the room, not the software: throw distance, projector lumens versus ambient light, where people stand, how many at once…
Proof it worked: 240 fps phone video of 20 trials: median motion-to-photon latency under 100 ms, recountable from the footage.
Biggest trap: Tracking collapses under projector light. Fix: move the camera off-axis, use the heavy model, add IR light with an IR webcam.

Save this for when you build it. The carousel is the map; the guide has every prompt.
